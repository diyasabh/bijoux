Privacy at Bijoux

Effective August 4, 2026

Bijoux is a keepsake app: people make little hand-drawn charms and give them to each other with magic words. It is built to know as little about you as possible.

What the app stores

  • Your username, and optionally your first and last name and a profile photo — so bijoux can say who they're from.
  • Bijoux you make or receive — their names, drawings, and magic words.
  • Your iCloud identity (an anonymous Apple identifier) — so your collection follows you to a new phone and so the app knows which seals are yours.

Where it lives

Everything is stored on your device and in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), under Apple's security and privacy protections. There are no Bijoux servers. We never see your data.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, no trackers, no ads, no third-party SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No reading your contacts, location, or messages.

Sharing between people

When you seal a bijou behind a magic word, that bijou (its drawing, name, and your username) is held in iCloud so the people you tell the word can receive it. Receiving is always deliberate: nothing arrives unless a person types the word themselves.

Deleting everything

In the app: your profile → delete my account & bijoux. This removes your bijoux, your standing seals (their words become free), your username, and your local data. There is no undo.
