Bijoux support

How giving works

Make a bijou, name it, and craft a magic word for it. Tell anyone the word — out loud, in a text, in a card. When they open Bijoux, tap receive, and speak the word, the bijou appears for them. A word stays locked to its bijou until you release it from your givings.

Common questions

“Nothing answered to that word.” Spelling and spacing matter — check the exact word with the person who gave it to you. Capitalization doesn't.

A bijou didn't arrive? Bijoux needs an internet connection (any — 5G is fine) and an iCloud account, which every iPhone normally has. Check Settings → your name → iCloud.

Want a word back? Your profile → your givings → release the word. The bijou returns to your dish and the word is free again.
